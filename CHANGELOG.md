## [0.1.0]

First release.

### Added
- Full-screen terminal interface with eight screens: dashboard, transactions, daily, categories, wallets, budget, search and trash.
- Income and expense categories with optional monthly quotas, and a monthly budget, with warnings that never block entry.
- Multiple wallets, transfers with optional fees, and lower and upper bound alerts.
- Pie charts per month and per day, quota and budget bars, wallet sparklines, and live previews in entry forms.
- Trash with restore and permanent delete.
- Search across all months, sorting, manual reordering, configurable key bindings.
- `export`, `backup`, `--check` and `--dump-keys` commands.
- Modular source tree, single-file build (`make dist`), man page and test suite.
